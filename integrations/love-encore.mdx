From Captivated, you can reschedule or cancel appointments, send customers new booking links, block unavailable time, adjust your booking availability, and quickly find customers based on their appointment status.

<Note>
  Love-Encore booking is configured around the customer's **Date of Delivery** to ensure appointments are offered within the appropriate eligibility window.
</Note>

## Reschedule an Appointment

There are two ways to reschedule an appointment:

1. **Choose the new appointment time for the customer** if you already know when they'd like to come in.
2. **Send the customer a reschedule link** so they can choose another available time themselves.

The reschedule booking link continues to honor the customer's original **Date of Delivery**, so they will only see appointments within their eligible booking window.

### Choose a New Time for the Customer

If the customer has already told you when they'd like to reschedule:

1. Find the blue **Appointment Booked** box within the conversation.
2. Select **Reschedule**.
3. Select **Select a new appointment time**.
4. Captivated will open your booking site in a new tab.
5. Select the customer's new date and time and complete the booking.

Once the new appointment is submitted:

- The Captivated Workflow moves to **Rescheduled**.
- The customer receives a text confirming their new appointment.
- The appointment on your connected Google or Outlook calendar automatically moves to the new date and time.

You do **not** need to manually move the calendar event.

![](https://play.vidyard.com/d9XrLiAdxcxwAbf2wZLuS8.jpg)

### Let the Customer Choose a New Time

If the customer doesn't know when they'd like to reschedule:

1. Find the blue **Appointment Booked** box.
2. Select **Reschedule**.
3. Choose **Send a reschedule link**.

![](https://learn.captivated.works/wp-content/uploads/2020/04/send-reschedule-link-1024x360.png)

Captivated will populate the reschedule message in the **Write a Message** field. Review the message and select **Send**.

The customer will receive a link allowing them to choose another eligible appointment time.

<Note>
  Sending a reschedule request **automatically cancels the customer's original appointment**.
</Note>

![](https://learn.captivated.works/wp-content/uploads/2020/04/Screenshot-2025-07-28-at-3.33.48%20PM-1024x587.png)

The Workflow will move to **Reschedule Requested** while you wait for the customer to select a new appointment.

### What Happens If They Don't Rebook?

Captivated will automatically follow up after the reschedule request:

- If the customer hasn't booked within **2 days**, Captivated sends a reminder.
- If the customer hasn't booked within **7 days**, the Workflow moves to **Final Reach Out**.
- Once in **Final Reach Out**, no additional automated messages are sent.

If you'd like to try again, you can manually send another booking request using the **three-dot More menu → Request Appointment**.

## Cancel an Appointment

When a customer asks to cancel, make sure you cancel the appointment through Captivated rather than simply leaving the appointment on the calendar.

Canceling through Captivated will:

- Remove the appointment from your connected Google or Outlook calendar
- Stop future automated appointment reminders
- Update the customer's Workflow status
- Send the customer a cancellation confirmation

To cancel:

1. Find the blue **Appointment Booked** box.
2. Select the red **Cancel** button.
3. Select **Cancel this appointment**.

![](https://play.vidyard.com/RFFKo3oMxAntsygkC3HAy6.jpg)

The Workflow will move to **Canceled**, and the customer will receive a confirmation text.

No additional automated appointment reminders are sent while the conversation is in the **Canceled** state.

If the customer later wants to schedule again, use the **three-dot More menu → Request Appointment**.

## Book an Appointment for a Customer Who Can't Use the Link

If a customer doesn't have a smartphone or would prefer that you complete the booking process for them, you can assist them.

Scan your dealership's Love-Encore QR code and complete the booking form **on behalf of the customer**.

<Tip>
  Complete the initial form rather than bypassing the process. The customer's **Date of Delivery** is used to determine their eligible booking window.
</Tip>

Once the form is submitted, continue through the normal booking process and select an available appointment for the customer.

## Block Time Off on Your Calendar

Your Captivated booking site uses your connected Google or Outlook calendar to determine when you're available.

If you're unavailable during hours when you would normally accept Love-Encore appointments, block that time on your connected calendar.

This includes:

- Vacation
- Meetings
- Personal appointments
- Training
- Other times you cannot accept a Love-Encore appointment

![](https://play.vidyard.com/aahznCSveNpJyx9RaZ5pTj.jpg)

### Google Calendar

If your organization uses a Google business account, choose **Out of Office** when available.

If **Out of Office** isn't available, create a regular calendar event and make sure it is marked **Busy**.

![](https://learn.captivated.works/wp-content/uploads/2020/04/Screen-Shot-2022-08-09-at-9.43.23-AM.png)

![](https://learn.captivated.works/wp-content/uploads/2020/04/busy.png)

### Outlook

Create an event for the unavailable time and make sure **Show As** is set to **Busy**.

<Warning>
  Your event must be marked **Busy**, not **Free**. Captivated will not treat an event marked Free as unavailable time.
</Warning>

If you edit an Outlook event, double-check its availability status before saving, as the setting can sometimes change.

After blocking your time, go to **Appointments → Add Event** in Captivated and verify that those dates and times are no longer available on your booking site.

## Adjust Your Booking Site Settings

You can manage when and how customers are allowed to schedule Love-Encore appointments.

In Captivated:

1. Go to **Appointments**.
2. Select your calendar from the dropdown.
3. Select **Settings**.

![](https://play.vidyard.com/rHvgjx1mXCGTmqcoydPzsC.jpg)

### Appointment Details

Under **Appointment Details**, you can adjust the duration of your Love-Encore appointments.

### Availability

Under **Availability**, set the days and hours when you'd like to accept Love-Encore appointments.

Additional scheduling options include:

- Buffer before appointments
- Buffer after appointments
- Minimum booking notice
- Limit future bookings
- Minimum cancellation notice

Hover over the **info icon** next to a setting for additional information.

### Advanced Settings

Under **Advanced**, you can adjust your available **time slot intervals**.

![](https://learn.captivated.works/wp-content/uploads/2020/04/Screenshot-2025-07-28-at-3.43.28%20PM.png)

<Check>
  Before making significant changes to your Love-Encore booking configuration or eligibility settings, contact Captivated Support if you're unsure how the change will affect customer scheduling.
</Check>

![](https://learn.captivated.works/wp-content/uploads/2020/04/Screenshot-2025-07-28-at-3.46.10%20PM-1024x470.png)

## Find Your Upcoming Appointments

Captivated's conversation filters make it easy to find customers based on where they are in the Love-Encore process.

1. Select the **Search** icon above your conversation list.
2. Choose the status you'd like to view.

For example, select **Upcoming** to see conversations with scheduled upcoming appointments.

You can also filter for other Love-Encore statuses, including customers who:

- Have not yet booked
- Have canceled
- Have completed their Redelivery
- Are in other stages of the appointment process

![](https://learn.captivated.works/wp-content/uploads/2020/04/Screenshot-2025-07-28-at-3.48.00%20PM-1024x467.png)

![](https://learn.captivated.works/wp-content/uploads/2020/04/Screen-Shot-2022-08-04-at-4.43.42-PM.png)

Choose "Upcoming" to filter and see all of your scheduled appointments within your conversation list. You can also filter to see all canceled appointments, Redelivered appointments, or conversations where the customer has not yet booked an appointment and more!

![](https://learn.captivated.works/wp-content/uploads/2020/04/Screen-Shot-2022-08-04-at-4.45.05-PM.png)

## Why Doesn't a Conversation Have an Appointment Status?

Your Love-Encore conversations should typically move through statuses such as **Appointment Requested**, **Booked**, **Reschedule Requested**, **Rescheduled**, or **Canceled** as customers move through the process.

If a conversation doesn't have the expected status, you may be using a custom Template or message that isn't connected to the appropriate Love-Encore automation.

Contact Captivated Support so we can review the Template and automation associated with your account.

<Tip>
  Don't manually try to correct the Workflow if you're unsure why a status is missing. Support can determine why the expected automation didn't occur.
</Tip>
