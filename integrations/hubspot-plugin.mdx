The Captivated HubSpot integration allows your team to access SMS conversations without leaving the contact record you're working from in HubSpot.

With the integration, you can:

- View existing Captivated conversations.
- Review SMS conversation history.
- Send new text messages.
- Choose which Captivated Channel to send from.
- Open the full conversation directly in Captivated.

## View Captivated Conversations in HubSpot

Once the Captivated HubSpot integration is installed, open a contact record in HubSpot and locate the **Captivated** section in the right-hand panel.

If the contact has existing SMS conversations in Captivated, they'll appear here.

From an existing conversation, you can:

- Select **Actions** to view the conversation within HubSpot.
- Select the **launch icon** next to the Channel to open the full conversation in Captivated.
- Review previous SMS messages with the contact.

Messages sent through HubSpot are also reflected in Captivated, keeping the conversation history together.

## Start a New Conversation

To send a new text message from a HubSpot contact record:

1. Locate the **Captivated** section on the contact record.
2. Select **Start a Conversation**.
3. Choose the Captivated **Channel** you'd like to send the message from.
4. Enter your message.
5. Select **Send**.

If your organization has multiple Channels, be sure to select the appropriate Channel for the conversation.

Once sent, the message will appear in Captivated immediately.

## Conversation History

You can review existing SMS conversation history from within the Captivated window in HubSpot.

For more advanced conversation management, select the **launch icon** to open the conversation directly in Captivated.

## Best Practices

- Make sure the HubSpot contact has an accurate mobile phone number before starting a conversation.
- Select the appropriate Channel when your organization uses multiple texting numbers.
- Continue using your organization's normal texting and consent practices when messaging contacts from HubSpot.
- Use Captivated when you need access to additional conversation management features that aren't available within the HubSpot integration.
