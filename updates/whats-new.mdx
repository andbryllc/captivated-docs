  ## Pinning Conversations

  You can now **pin conversations** to keep important or frequently accessed threads at the top of your Conversations list.

  Pinned conversations are private to you and can be accessed anytime from the dedicated **Pins** view in the Messages menu.

  [Learn how to pin, organize, and manage your conversations](\(https://captivatedllc.mintlify.app/features/pinning-conversations\))
</Update>

<Update label="July 7, 2026">
  ## 🚨 New Feature: AI Conversation Summaries

  Quickly catch up on customer conversations with AI-powered summaries! When an eligible SMS conversation is completed, Captivated automatically generates a concise recap of the interaction, making it easier to review conversation history and keep your team informed.

  AI Conversation Summaries is a premium, usage-based feature. [Learn more about AI Summaries](ai-conversation-summaries-and-bubble-based-scrolling).
</Update>

<Update label="July 2025">
  ## **New Features: Global Search, Status Page & Announcements**

  We've introduced Contacts Global Search, System Status, and a dedicated Announcements view to help you find information and manage customer communications more efficiently.

  - **Contacts Global Search:** Quickly find contacts by name, phone number, or email address using the search bar at the top of Captivated.
  - **System Status:** Check Captivated's system status and subscribe to updates through the [System Status](https://captivated.statuspage.io/) option in the Support menu.
  - **Announcements View:** Find and manage announcements separately from your text conversations, with filters for scheduled, active, and completed announcements.

  Learn more about [Announcements](https://learn.captivated.works/new-features-global-search-status-page-announcements/) here. 
</Update>
