## What Is a Channel?

A **channel** is an avenue customers can use to communicate with your business through Captivated.

Depending on your account, your channels may include:

- **SMS Channels** — Phone numbers customers can call or text.
- **Website Texts** — Conversations started through the texting option on your website.
- **Live Chat** — Live chat conversations started from your website, if enabled.
- **Secure Chat** — Secure conversations used to exchange sensitive information.

Each channel can have its own routing, permissions, compliance settings, and other preferences.

## View Your Channels

To view the channels associated with your account:

1. Select the **gear icon** to open Administration.
2. Select **Channels**.

The Channels page provides an overview of your channels and important information about each one, including:

- Channel name and type
- Where incoming conversations are routed (user or queue)
- Assigned campaign
- Brand name

Use the **filters** to narrow your channels by type, campaign, routing destination, or brand. You can also search for a specific channel by name.

## Understand Channel Routing

Each channel determines where a new inbound conversation is sent.

A channel can route to a [**Queue**](https://app.mintlify.com/captivatedllc/captivatedllc/~/6b581c77-bf91-4c70-9611-f0cb28bb2a12) or directly to a [**User**.](https://app.mintlify.com/captivatedllc/captivatedllc/~/d2255645-aad8-4cca-b2bc-2b1c9d73c8e5)

| Routing Option | What Happens |
| :-- | :-- |
| [**Queue**](https://app.mintlify.com/captivatedllc/captivatedllc/~/6b581c77-bf91-4c70-9611-f0cb28bb2a12) | New inbound conversations are sent to a group of designated Queue Operators who can respond to the customer. |
| [**User**](https://app.mintlify.com/captivatedllc/captivatedllc/~/d2255645-aad8-4cca-b2bc-2b1c9d73c8e5) | New inbound conversations are routed directly to the selected user. |

If a channel routes to a Queue, you'll see the Queue associated with that channel. If it routes directly to a user, you'll see the user's name.

## View Channel Details

Select a **channel name** to view its configuration and details.

Depending on the channel type, you may see:

- Phone number

## Registration Link & QR Code

SMS channels may include a unique [**Registration Link and QR Code**](https://app.mintlify.com/captivatedllc/captivatedllc/editor/main/~/600c370b-008e-45b7-b7f2-63a150a70172) that can be used to collect customer opt-ins.

You can add your Registration Link or QR Code to places such as:

- Websites
- Email communications
- Printed materials
- Customer onboarding materials
- In-store signage

To access them:

1. Open **Administration \> Channels**.
2. Select the channel.
3. Locate the **Registration Link** or **QR Code**.
4. Copy the Registration Link or preview and download the QR Code.

### How Registration Works

When a customer registers through your Registration Link or QR Code, Captivated initiates the opt-in process. The customer must complete the required confirmation to finish registering for text communications.<br />

<Warning>
  Registration and consent requirements help protect your business and your customers. Don't modify or bypass the opt-in process.
</Warning>

Administrators can review applicable opt-in language and settings under **Administration \> Compliance Profiles**.

## Manage Channel Access

The **Sharing** settings determine which users can send outbound messages from a particular channel.

You can provide access to:

- An entire [Team](https://app.mintlify.com/captivatedllc/captivatedllc/~/fb7c6b33-6dac-4e56-b948-6e640c1b813b)
- Individual [Users](https://app.mintlify.com/captivatedllc/captivatedllc/~/d2255645-aad8-4cca-b2bc-2b1c9d73c8e5)

### Add Channel Access

1. Open the channel.
2. Select **Sharing**.
3. Choose **Add Another Team or User**.
4. Select the Team or User.
5. Save your changes.

### Remove Channel Access

Remove the Team or User from the Sharing section and save your changes.

<Note>
  Channel sharing controls who can **send outbound messages from the channel**. It does not determine where new inbound conversations are routed.
</Note>

## Edit Channel Settings

Administrators can select **Edit** to manage available channel settings.

Depending on your channel type and account configuration, these may include:

- **Channel Name**
- **Routing Destination**
- **Inbound Call Forwarding**
- **Signature Preferences**
- **Compliance Profile**
- **Brand Name and Registration Information**
- **Unjoin Users When Conversations Are Completed**

### Unjoin Users When Conversations Are Completed

When this setting is enabled, users are automatically unjoined when a conversation is completed.

If that customer texts again later, the new inbound message follows the channel's routing rules rather than automatically returning to the users who previously participated in the conversation.

## Add or Remove a Channel
