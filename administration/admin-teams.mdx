## What Is a Team?

A **Team** is a group of Captivated users. Teams make it easier to organize users and manage settings or permissions for multiple people at once.

For example, your organization might create separate Teams for:

- Sales
- Customer Service
- Service
- Billing
- Management

Users can be added to the appropriate Teams based on their role within your organization.

## View Your Teams

To view and manage your Teams:

1. Select the **gear icon** to open Administration.
2. Select **Teams**.

From here, you can view your existing Teams or use the search bar to find a specific Team.

<Tip>
  Enter at least three characters when searching for a Team.
</Tip>

## Create a Team

To create a new Team:

1. Select **\+ Add Team**.
2. Enter a name for the Team.
3. Select **Save**.

Once your Team is created, you can add the appropriate users and configure any available Team settings.

### Add Users to a Team

After creating a Team, open it to manage its membership.

Assign each user the appropriate Team Role based on the level of conversation visibility they need.

Users can belong to multiple Teams, and their role can differ between Teams.

## Why Use Teams?

Teams make account management easier by allowing you to manage groups of users instead of configuring each person individually.

For example, Teams can be used when managing access to certain [Channels](/administration/admin-channels) or determining who can transfer conversations to specific [Queues](/administration/admin-queues).

## Team Roles & Permissions

When adding a user to a Team, you can assign a **Team Role** that determines what they're allowed to see within that Team.

A user's Team Role is separate from their overall Captivated account permissions and other access, such as Channels and Queues.

| Team Role | Conversation Visibility |
| :-- | :-- |
| **Member** | Conversations they initiate, conversations transferred to them by other Team members, and conversations they're added to using the **Members** button. |
| **Manager / Administrator** | All conversations across that Team. |

![](https://learn.captivated.works/wp-content/uploads/2020/05/Screenshot-2024-06-07-at-12.01.45%20PM.png)

<Note>
  Being assigned as a **Team Administrator** does not automatically grant access to Captivated's **Administration** section. Organization-level administrative permissions are managed separately.
</Note>

<Note>
  A user can belong to multiple Teams and may have a different role within each Team.
</Note>
