title: "Account Usage & Billing"
description: "View your account usage, access billing information, and manage who can view your account usage."
---

Captivated Administrators can view current account usage and billing information from the **Account Usage** section.

## View Your Account Usage

To access your usage:

1. Select the **gear icon** to open Administration.
2. Select **Account Usage**.

Here you can review your account's current usage and balance.

Usage may include:

- Text message usage
- Voice minutes
- Text lines
- Users
- Phone number lookups
- Video chat participant minutes
- Current account balance
- AI Summaries
- RCS message usage

<Note>
  Usage information typically updates once per day and reflects activity within your current billing cycle.
</Note>

## Manage Your Subscription & Billing

Select **Manage Subscription** to access the billing portal.

From the billing portal, you can manage available billing options, including:

- View invoices
- Review billing history
- Update your payment method
- Manage other available subscription or billing settings

<Note>
  The options available in the billing portal may vary based on your account and subscription.
</Note>

## Share Account Usage

Administrators automatically have access to the **Usage** section.

If you'd like other members of your team to view account usage information, you can grant access from the **Sharing** tab.

### Add Access

1. Open the **Sharing** tab.
2. Select **\+ Add Another Team or User**.
3. Choose the team or user you'd like to add.
4. Select **Save**.

### Remove Access

To remove a non-administrator's access, select the **trash icon** next to their name and save your changes.

<Warning>
  Account usage may contain billing and subscription information. Only share access with team members who need visibility into this information.
</Warning>
