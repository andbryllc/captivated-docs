## Logging In to Captivated

To sign in to Captivated, open your preferred web browser and visit [**app.captivated.works**](http://app.captivated.works).

- Enter your email address and password.
- Select **Log In**.
- Bookmark the login page for quick access in the future.

If you're unsure which email address is associated with your account, contact your manager or reach out to **Captivated Support**.

# Two-Factor Authentication (2FA)

If your account has **Two-Factor Authentication (2FA)** enabled, you'll receive a **6-digit verification code** via text message after entering your email address and password.

Enter the verification code to complete the sign-in process.

# Logging Out

To sign out of Captivated:

1. Select your **profile avatar**
2. Click **Log Out**.

For your security, Captivated will automatically sign you out after **12 hours** of inactivity or when your session expires.

# Browser Compatibility

Captivated supports the latest versions of:

- ✅ Google Chrome _(recommended)_
- ✅ Mozilla Firefox
- ✅ Apple Safari

**Internet Explorer is not supported.**

# Mobile App

The Captivated mobile app keeps you signed in for convenience. If the app hasn't been used for approximately **two weeks**, you'll be asked to sign in again.

# Need Help?

If you're having trouble accessing your account, we're here to help.

- Chat with **Captivated Support** from within the application.
- Text **Captivated Support** at **(317) 316-3600**.
