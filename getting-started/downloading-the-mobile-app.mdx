---
title: "Downloading the mobile app"
description: "How to download and sign in to the Captivated mobile app so you can work from anywhere."
---

Stay connected to your customers wherever you are with the **Captivated** mobile app.

Download the app from the **Apple App Store** or **Google Play Store** by searching for **"Captivated Texting."** Look for the **blue icon with the white chat boxes.**

<Frame>
  ![Screenshot 2026 08 06 At 4 04 20 PM](/images/Screenshot-2026-08-06-at-4.04.20-PM.png "Screenshot 2026 08 06 At 4 04 20 PM")
</Frame>

Once installed, sign in using the same email address and password you use for your Captivated account.

<Tip>
  Push notifications help you respond to customers faster and ensure you never miss an important conversation while you're away from your desk.
</Tip>

Captivated's mobile app syncs to the cloud, so anything that happens on the web application (on your browser) also happens on the mobile application and vice versa.

<Tip>
  On your phone's home screen, move Captivated close to your regular texting app so that you have both your business and personal texting close at hand.
</Tip>

<Warning>
  The mobile app will log you out after a period of about 2 weeks with no activity.
</Warning>
