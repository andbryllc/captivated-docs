Your signature helps customers identify who they're communicating with. You can customize your signature and choose how often it's included in your text messages.

## Access Your Signature Settings

1. Select your **profile avatar**. 
2. Choose **My Profile**.
3. Scroll to the **Signature** section.

From here, you can edit your signature and choose when it should be included in your messages.

## Signature Options

### Use Default

**Use Default** applies your organization's default signature settings.

Depending on how your account is configured:

- If your administrator has created a company-wide signature, that signature will be used.
- If no company-wide signature exists, your signature will default to your first and last name.

> ~ Erin Hannon

You can also customize your signature by entering your own text in the **Signature** field.

> ~ Erin | Dunder Mifflin

# When Sender Changes (Recommended)

This option includes your signature:

- On the first message you send in a conversation.
- Whenever a different team member joins the conversation.

For example:

- Erin sends the first message → **~ Erin**
- Dwight replies → **~ Dwight**
- Erin responds again after Dwight → **~ Erin**

This helps customers know who they're speaking with without adding a signature to every message.

### Always

Your signature is included with **every** text message you send.

This option is useful if you always want customers to know who sent each message.

### Never

Your signature is never included in your outgoing text messages.

## Best Practices

For most businesses, **When Sender Changes** provides the best customer experience. It clearly identifies who is participating in the conversation while keeping messages clean and easy to read.

If your organization uses a company-wide signature, check with your administrator before making changes to ensure your signature follows your company's branding guidelines.
