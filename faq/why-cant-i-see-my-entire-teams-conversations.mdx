---
title: "Why can't I see my entire team's conversations?"
description: "Learn how conversation filters and user permissions determine which conversations you can see in Captivated."
---

If you're only seeing your own conversations, first check your **conversation filters**.

## Check Your Conversation Filters

Your conversation list may currently be filtered to show only conversations associated with you.

To view conversations across your organization:

1. Open **Conversations**.
2. Change the conversation filter from [**Mine** to **Everyone's Conversations**](/features/conversation-filtering-and-searching).
3. Adjust your conversation status filter, such as **Active** or **All**, depending on what you're looking for.

Learn more about [filtering and searching conversations](/features/conversation-filtering-and-searching).

## Still Can't See Other Conversations?

If you've selected **Everyone's Conversations** but still can't see conversations you aren't assigned or joined to, check your Captivated **Account Role**.

| Account Role | Conversation Access |
| :-- | :-- |
| **Member** | Can view conversations they initiate or are joined to. |
| **Manager** | Can view conversations across the organization, including conversations they aren't assigned or joined to. |
| **Administrator** | Can view conversations across the organization and also has access to administrative account settings. |

Changing your filter to **Everyone's Conversations** does not give you additional permissions. It only displays conversations your Account Role already allows you to access.

> **Example:** If you're a Member, switching from **Mine** to **Everyone's Conversations** will not allow you to see conversations you don't already have access to.

## Need Additional Access?

Your Captivated Administrator can review your Account Role and determine whether you should have **Manager** or **Administrator** access.

Learn more about [User Roles & Permissions](/administration/admin-users).

## Need Help?

If your Account Role appears correct but you're still unable to see the conversations you need, contact **Captivated Support** using the in-app **Support** option or text **(317) 316-3600**.
