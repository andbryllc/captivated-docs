---
title: "Why can't I see new incoming messages?"
description: "Learn how Queue Operators can take ownership of new incoming conversations and begin responding to customers."
---

When a new incoming message routes to a [Queue](/administration/admin-queues), it remains unassigned until a **Queue Operator** takes ownership of the conversation.

## Take a Conversation

If you're a Queue Operator:

1. Locate the new queued conversation above your conversation list or from the Dashboard.
2. Select the **Take** option to take ownership of the conversation.
3. Once you've taken the conversation, you become the **conversation owner**.
4. Respond to the customer or [transfer the conversation](/features/conversations-transferring) to the appropriate User or Queue.

Once you take ownership, the conversation is removed from the Queue so other Operators know it's being handled.

## Not Seeing New Queued Conversations?

If you aren't seeing incoming conversations for a Queue, you may not be assigned as a **Queue Operator**.

Your Captivated Administrator can review the Queue's Operators and add you if needed.

Learn more about [Queues and Queue Operators](/administration/admin-queues).

<Note>
  Being a member of a Team does not automatically make you an Operator of that Team's Queue. Queue Operators are configured separately.
</Note>

## Need Help?

If you're a Queue Operator but aren't seeing incoming conversations as expected, contact **Captivated Support** using the in-app **Support** option or text **(317) 316-3600**.
