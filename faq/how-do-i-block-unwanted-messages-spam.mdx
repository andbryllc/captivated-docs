title: "How do I block unwanted messages/spam?"
description: "Learn how to block a contact from sending unwanted messages or spam in Captivated."
---

Receiving unwanted messages from a contact? You can block the contact to prevent additional incoming messages from reaching you in Captivated.

For a video walkthrough and step-by-step instructions, view our [Blocking a Contact guide](https://captivatedllc.mintlify.site/features/blocking-a-contact).

Questions? Contact Captivated Support using the **Support** option in the left-hand menu or text **317.316.3600**.
