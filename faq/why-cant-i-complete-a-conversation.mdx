---
title: "Why can't I complete a conversation?"
description: "Learn why the Complete option may not be available and how conversation ownership affects your available actions."
---

If you don't see the option to **Complete** a conversation, it's most likely because you are **not the conversation owner**.

Only the owner of a conversation can complete it.

## Owner vs. Joined Member

Your available conversation actions depend on whether you own the conversation or are simply joined as a member.

| Your Role | Available Action |
| :-- | :-- |
| **Conversation Owner** | Can complete the conversation. |
| **Joined Member** | Can leave the conversation but cannot complete it. |

If you're a joined member, selecting **Leave** removes you from the conversation and from your active conversation list.

## Need to Complete the Conversation Yourself?

If you're not currently the owner but need to take responsibility for and complete the conversation, use the **Transfer** option to transfer ownership to yourself.

Once you're the conversation owner, you'll have the option to complete it.

<Tip>
  If you no longer need to participate in the conversation and someone else owns it, you don't need to take ownership just to remove it from your list. Simply **Leave** the conversation instead.
</Tip>

Learn more about [transferring, joining, leaving, and completing conversations](/features/conversations-transferring).

## What About Internal Group Chats?

Internal [Group Chats](/premium-features/team-chat) work differently from customer conversations.

Only the user who originally created the Group Chat can complete it. Other participants cannot transfer ownership to themselves in order to complete the conversation.

If you no longer need to participate in the Group Chat, ask the creator to remove you when necessary.

## Need Help?

If you're unable to complete, leave, or transfer a conversation as expected, contact **Captivated Support** using the in-app **Support** option or text **(317) 316-3600**.
