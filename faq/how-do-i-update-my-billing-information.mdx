---
title: "How do I update my billing information?"
description: "Learn how to update the credit card on file for your Captivated subscription."
---

You can update the credit card on file for your Captivated subscription in either of these locations:

- **Administration → View Account Usage → Manage Subscription**
- **My Account → Manage Subscription**

From there, follow the prompts to securely update your payment information.

**Don't see the Manage Subscription option?** Contact Captivated Support using the **Support** option in the left-hand menu. Our team can send you a secure link to update the card on file.
