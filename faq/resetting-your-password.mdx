title: "How do I reset my password?"
description: "Reset a forgotten Captivated password or change your password while you're logged in."
---

Whether you've forgotten your password or simply want to change it, there are several ways to update your Captivated login credentials.

## Forgot Your Password?

If you're unable to log in:

1. Go to the Captivated login page at [**app.captivated.works**](http://app.captivated.works).
2. Select **Forgot Your Password?**
3. Enter the email address associated with your Captivated account.
4. Choose **Reset via Email** or **Reset via SMS**.
5. Follow the instructions to create your new password.

### Reset via Email

Select **Reset via Email** to receive a password reset link at the email address associated with your Captivated account.

Look for an email with the subject **Password Reset Request** and select **Reset Your Password**.

Follow the instructions to create a new password.

### Reset via SMS

Select **Reset via SMS** to receive a verification code by text message.

Enter the code you receive and follow the instructions to create your new password.

<Note>
  Reset via SMS is only available if your personal mobile number has been added to the **Mobile Phone Number** field in your Captivated profile.
</Note>

## Ask Your Administrator to Send a Password Reset

If you're unable to reset your password yourself, a Captivated Administrator within your organization can send you a password reset email.

Administrators can manage password resets from **Administration \> Users**.

Learn more about [Managing Users](/administration/admin-users).

## Change Your Password While Logged In

If you know your current password and simply want to change it:

1. Open **My Profile**.
2. Select **Password and Credentials**.
3. Choose **Change Your Password**.
4. Enter your current password.
5. Enter your new password.
6. Select **Change Password**.

## Password Requirements

Your new password must be at least **8 characters long**.

For better account security, use a password that is unique to Captivated and difficult for others to guess.

<Tip>
  Consider using a password manager to create and securely store a unique password.
</Tip>

## Having Trouble Logging In After a Password Reset?

If you've successfully reset your password but still can't log in:

- Make sure you're using your **new password**, not your previous one.
- If your browser or password manager automatically fills in your password, make sure it hasn't inserted an old password.
- Manually enter your new password and try again.
- Confirm you're using the email address associated with your Captivated account.
- If you're still unable to log in, contact your Captivated Administrator or Captivated Support.

<Tip>
  **New to Captivated?** If you haven't completed your initial account setup yet, use the invitation email sent when your account was created rather than the standard password reset process.
</Tip>
