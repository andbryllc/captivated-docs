---
title: "Authentication Policy"
---

Authentication Policy allows Administrators to control how Users within their organization sign in to Captivated.

You can allow your team to sign in using a **Captivated password, Google account, Microsoft/Outlook account, or a combination of these methods**.

For organizations using Google Workspace or Microsoft 365, this provides a convenient way for employees to access Captivated using their existing accounts.

<Note>
  Authentication Policy settings are available to organization Administrators when this feature is enabled for their account. If you don't see these settings, contact Captivated Support.
</Note>

## Access Your Authentication Policy

To view or update your organization's sign-in settings:

1. Sign in to Captivated as an Administrator.
2. Navigate to **Administration → Organization Settings**.
3. Locate **Authentication Policy**.

These settings apply to all Users within your organization.

## Choose Your Sign-In Methods

Authentication Policy provides two configuration options.

### Automatic

When **Automatic** is enabled, Users can choose from all available sign-in methods:

- Google
- Password
- Microsoft/Outlook

After entering their email address, Users can select their preferred sign-in method.

### Select Identity Providers

To restrict which sign-in methods your organization allows:

1. Turn **Automatic** off.
2. Enable the Identity Providers you want to allow:
   - **Google**
   - **Password**
   - **Microsoft/Outlook**
3. Save your changes.

Your team will only be offered the sign-in methods you've enabled.

For example:

- **Google + Password:** Users can choose between Google and their Captivated password.
- **Microsoft/Outlook only:** Users are directed to Microsoft sign-in after entering their email.
- **Google only:** Users are directed to Google sign-in without an additional selection screen.

<Check>
  Important: At least one Identity Provider must remain enabled when Automatic is turned off. Captivated will not allow you to save a configuration that disables every sign-in method.
</Check>

## What Your Team Sees When Signing In

All Users begin by entering their email address on the Captivated login screen.

What happens next depends on your organization's Authentication Policy.

| Authentication Policy | Sign-In Experience |
| :-- | :-- |
| Automatic enabled | All available sign-in options are displayed. |
| Multiple Identity Providers enabled | Users choose from the enabled methods. |
| One Identity Provider enabled | Users are directed straight to that sign-in method. |

### Signing In on Mobile

If your organization uses Google or Microsoft/Outlook sign-in, make sure you're using the **latest version of the Captivated mobile app**.

Older app versions may not reliably support these sign-in methods.

If you're having trouble signing in with Google or Microsoft on your mobile device:

1. Open the Apple App Store or Google Play Store.
2. Check for an available Captivated app update.
3. Install the latest version.
4. Try signing in again.

> **Tip:** We recommend updating the Captivated mobile app before your Administrator changes your organization's Authentication Policy to Google or Microsoft-only sign-in.

The same Authentication Policy applies when signing in through Captivated on desktop or mobile.

## Disabling Password Sign-In

Organizations that prefer employees to use Google or Microsoft can disable traditional Captivated password sign-in.

When **Password** is disabled, password-related options are automatically removed because they are no longer applicable.

Administrators will no longer see these User management actions:

- Resend Invitation
- Send Password Reset
- Force Password Reset

Individual Users will no longer see **Change Your Password** under their Credentials settings or the password option in their mobile profile.

<Check>
  Important: Before disabling Password, confirm that your employees can successfully access the Google or Microsoft accounts associated with their Captivated email addresses.
</Check>

> **Important:** Before disabling Password, confirm that your employees can successfully access the Google or Microsoft accounts associated with their Captivated email addresses.

If Password is enabled again, the applicable password-management options become available.

<Info>
  Existing organizations can continue signing in as usual unless an Administrator changes their Authentication Policy. New organizations default to Password sign-in.
</Info>

## Terminal Users and Shared Workstations

Terminal Users are not affected by Authentication Policy settings.

Employees using a shared Terminal User account will continue accessing Captivated through their assigned PINs, regardless of which Identity Providers your organization allows.

## View Authentication Policy Changes

Administrators can see when the organization's Authentication Policy was last updated and who made the change.

This information appears under **Administration → Organization Settings**.

If Captivated makes an update on your organization's behalf, the change will be attributed to **Captivated Support**.

## Frequently Asked Questions

<Accordion title="Can different Users have different sign-in requirements?">
  No. Authentication Policy applies to the entire organization, rather than individual Users.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Can we allow both Google and Microsoft sign-in?">
  Yes. Turn off Automatic and enable both Google and Microsoft/Outlook under Identity Providers.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Why don't I see the password reset option?">
  Your organization may have disabled Password as an approved sign-in method. Contact your Administrator to confirm your organization's Authentication Policy.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="What happens if we enable only one sign-in method?">
  After entering their email address, Users are directed to the single enabled Identity Provider without having to select from multiple options.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Will this affect our Terminal Users?">
  No. Terminal Users continue using their existing PIN-based access.
</Accordion>

## Need Help?

If you have questions about Authentication Policy, need help configuring your organization's sign-in methods, or don't see the setting available, contact **Captivated Support** using the **Support** option in the left-hand menu.
