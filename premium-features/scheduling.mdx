Captivated's Calendar & Scheduling feature makes it easy to manage appointments, share availability, and allow customers to book appointments without the back-and-forth of coordinating schedules.

Connect your Google or Microsoft calendar to Captivated to offer real-time availability, send booking requests through conversations, and manage appointments in one place.

## What Can You Do With Calendar & Scheduling?

With Captivated's scheduling tools, you can:

- Connect your Google or Microsoft calendar to manage availability.
- Create booking pages that customers can use to schedule appointments.
- Set specific days and hours when appointments can be booked.
- Send appointment requests directly through Captivated conversations.
- Schedule appointments on behalf of customers.
- Automatically send appointment confirmations and reminders.
- Collect additional customer information during the booking process.
- Reschedule or cancel existing appointments.
- View upcoming appointments from the Calendar tab.
- Embed a booking page on your website.

## Connect Your Calendar

Before customers can book appointments, you'll need to connect your calendar to Captivated.

Captivated supports Google and Microsoft calendars.

1. Locate the email inviting you to connect your calendar.
2. Choose **Google** or **Microsoft**.
3. Follow the prompts to authorize Captivated to access your calendar.
4. Complete the connection process.

Once connected, Captivated can use your calendar availability when customers book appointments.

<Note>
  Connecting your calendar helps prevent customers from booking appointments during times when you're already unavailable. You can also configure specific booking hours to control when appointments can be scheduled.
</Note>

## Configure Your Booking Availability

Your booking availability determines which appointment times customers can select.

You can use your connected calendar to reflect existing commitments while defining the days and hours you want to make available for appointments.

To manage your availability:

1. Open **Appointments.**
2. Choose the calendar drop-down for the site you'd like to update
3. Select Settings > Availability
4. Configure the days and hours when appointments should be available.
5. Review your settings and save your changes.

<Check>
  Review your availability regularly to ensure customers are only offered appointment times that work with your schedule.
</Check>

## Customize Your Booking Page

Your booking page is where customers select an available appointment date and time.

Captivated allows you to customize the booking experience to better reflect your business.

Depending on your configuration, you can:

- Display your business logo.
- Customize booking page colors.
- Define available appointment times.
- Request additional information from customers before confirming an appointment.

For example, you may want to collect a company name or other information relevant to the appointment.

Information submitted through the booking form is available within the associated Captivated conversation.

## Send an Appointment Request

You can send a booking request directly to a customer through a Captivated conversation.

This allows the customer to select an appointment time based on your availability.

1. Open the customer's conversation in Captivated.
2. Compose your message.
3. Select the three-line menu > request an appointment
4. Send the message to the customer.
5. The customer can open the booking page, select an available date and time, complete any required fields, and confirm the appointment.

Once the appointment is booked, Captivated displays an **Appointment Booked** entry directly within the conversation.

This makes it easy to reference the appointment details without leaving the conversation.

### Use Templates for Appointment Requests

If you frequently send appointment requests, consider creating a reusable [Message Template](/features/templates).

Templates can help you maintain consistent messaging and save time when sharing booking information with customers.

## Schedule an Appointment on a Customer's Behalf

You can also book an appointment directly from a conversation without asking the customer to complete the booking process.

This is especially helpful when speaking with someone on the phone who has already confirmed their preferred appointment date and time.

To schedule an appointment:

1. Open the customer's conversation.
2. Select the **three-dot menu** at the bottom of the conversation.
3. Choose **Schedule Appointment**.
4. Select the booking site you'd like to use.
5. Choose the customer's preferred available date and time.
6. Review the customer information that has been prefilled.
7. Complete any additional required fields.
8. Submit the appointment.

Once the appointment is booked, an **Appointment Booked** entry appears within the conversation.

Any configured appointment confirmation messages will also be prepared according to your scheduling settings.

<Tip>
  Scheduling on a customer's behalf is useful when confirming appointments during phone calls or when a customer prefers assistance with booking.
</Tip>

## Appointment Confirmations and Reminders

Captivated can automatically send appointment confirmations and reminders to help customers stay informed.

Depending on your configuration, appointment notifications can be delivered by:

- **Text message (SMS)**
- **Email**

You can customize appointment confirmation and reminder messaging to match your business needs.

These notifications help customers understand when their appointment is scheduled and who they'll be meeting with.

<Note>
  Appointment confirmations and reminders are based on your configured scheduling settings. Review these settings to ensure the correct messages are being sent.
</Note>

## View Appointment Details in Conversations

When a customer books an appointment, Captivated displays an **Appointment Booked** entry within the associated conversation.

This entry allows you to reference appointment information, including details submitted during the booking process.

Keeping appointment information within the conversation makes it easier for your team to manage customer communication and scheduling together.

## Reschedule or Cancel an Appointment

If an appointment needs to change, you can manage it directly through Captivated.

1. Open the conversation containing the appointment.
2. Locate the **Appointment Booked** entry.
3. Select **Update**.
4. Follow the available options to reschedule or cancel the appointment.

When an appointment is canceled or rescheduled, Captivated can automatically notify the customer by text message.

<Warning>
  Before making changes, verify that you've selected the correct appointment. Review the updated appointment details to ensure the customer receives accurate information.
</Warning>

## View Upcoming Appointments

The **Calendar** tab provides a centralized place to review and manage your scheduled appointments.

Use the available **List View** to see upcoming appointments and keep track of your schedule.

You can also return to your calendar and booking page settings when you need to adjust availability or other scheduling preferences.

## Add Scheduling to Your Website

You can make it easier for customers to book appointments by adding your Captivated booking page to your website.

Your booking page can be shared directly or embedded into your website, allowing visitors to select available appointment times without contacting your team first.

This can be especially helpful for businesses that want to offer convenient, self-service scheduling.

## Best Practices for Scheduling

To get the most out of Captivated's Calendar & Scheduling feature:

- **Keep your calendar connected and updated.** Accurate availability helps prevent scheduling conflicts.
- **Review your booking hours.** Make sure the appointment times offered to customers align with your business operations.
- **Use appointment reminders.** Automated reminders help customers remember upcoming appointments.
- **Collect relevant information.** Add booking form fields that help your team prepare for appointments.
- **Use templates.** Standardize appointment requests to save time and maintain consistent communication.
- **Review appointments regularly.** Use the Calendar tab to stay informed about upcoming bookings.
