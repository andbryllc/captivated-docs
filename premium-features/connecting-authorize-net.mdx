Captivated supports credit card payments through an Authorize.Net payment gateway.

If your business uses Authorize.Net, you can connect your gateway to Captivated to request and collect credit card payments from customers.

**Looking to accept bank payments (ACH)?** Captivated also offers Express Pay, which supports credit card and ACH payments through Stripe and Plaid.

For more information, see [Express Pay](/premium-features/express-pay).

## Before You Begin

To connect Authorize.Net to Captivated, you'll need:

- An active merchant services account with a Merchant Service Provider (MSP).
- An Authorize.Net payment gateway account.
- Your Authorize.Net **API Login ID**.
- Your Authorize.Net **Transaction Key**.
- Access to your organization's payment provider settings in Captivated.

<Note>
  If you don't already have an Authorize.Net account, contact your Merchant Service Provider to get started.
</Note>

## Step 1: Contact Your Merchant Service Provider

Before setting up your Authorize.Net gateway, contact your Merchant Service Provider (MSP) and request a **VAR sheet**.

A VAR sheet contains the merchant processing information needed to configure your payment gateway.

Your Merchant Service Provider can help you obtain this information and confirm that your merchant account is ready to connect with Authorize.Net.

## Step 2: Set Up Your Authorize.Net Gateway

Once you have the necessary merchant processing information:

1. Visit [Authorize.Net](https://www.authorize.net/).
2. Create or access your Authorize.Net payment gateway account.
3. Complete the gateway setup using the information provided by your Merchant Service Provider.
4. Confirm that your gateway is active and ready to process payments.

<Note>
  If you need assistance creating or activating your gateway, contact Authorize.Net or your Merchant Service Provider.
</Note>

## Step 3: Locate Your Authorize.Net API Credentials

Captivated uses your Authorize.Net API credentials to connect to your payment gateway.

1. Sign in to your Authorize.Net Merchant Interface.
2. Navigate to **Account → Settings → API Credentials & Keys**.
3. Locate your **API Login ID**.
4. Generate a **Transaction Key** if you don't already have one available.
5. Securely retain both credentials for the next step.

<Warning>
  Your API Login ID and Transaction Key are sensitive payment credentials. Do not share them through regular text messages or unsecured email.

  If you generate a new Transaction Key, verify whether it will affect any existing integrations using your Authorize.Net gateway.
</Warning>

## Step 4: Connect Authorize.Net in Captivated

Once you have your Authorize.Net credentials:

1. Sign in to Captivated.
2. Navigate to **Administration → Payment Providers**.
3. Select **Add Payment Provider**.
4. Choose **Authorize.Net**.
5. Enter your **API Login ID** and **Transaction Key** in the corresponding fields.
6. Save your payment provider settings.

Your Authorize.Net gateway is now configured in Captivated.

[SCREENSHOT: Captivated Add Payment Provider screen with Authorize.Net selected]

## Step 5: Verify Your Payment Setup

Before requesting payments from customers:

1. Confirm that your Authorize.Net payment provider appears in Captivated.
2. Verify that your gateway credentials were entered correctly.
3. Confirm that your merchant account and payment gateway are active.
4. Follow your organization's payment testing procedures before processing live customer payments.

Once your setup is complete, you can use **Express Pay** to send secure payment requests through Captivated.

For instructions on requesting and managing payments, see [Express Pay](/premium-features/express-pay).

## Frequently Asked Questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Do I need an Authorize.Net account to use this integration?">
    Yes. You'll need an Authorize.Net payment gateway account connected to your merchant processing services.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What is a VAR sheet?">
    A VAR sheet contains information about your merchant processing account that may be required when configuring your Authorize.Net gateway. Your Merchant Service Provider can provide it.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Where do I find my API Login ID and Transaction Key?">
    Sign in to your Authorize.Net Merchant Interface and navigate to **Account → Settings → API Credentials & Keys**.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What if my payment provider isn't connecting?">
    Confirm that your API Login ID and Transaction Key are correct and that your Authorize.Net gateway is active.

    If you're still experiencing issues, contact Captivated Support for assistance.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Need Help?

For assistance connecting Authorize.Net to Captivated, contact **Captivated Support** using the Support option in the left-hand menu.

For questions about your merchant account, VAR sheet, or Authorize.Net gateway activation, contact your Merchant Service Provider or Authorize.Net directly.
