## Start a Video Chat

To initiate a Video Chat:

1. Open the conversation with the contact.
2. Select the **three-dot menu** below the message field.
3. Choose **Invite to Video Chat**.
4. Review the message that populates and make any changes if needed.
5. Select **Send**.

The recipient will receive a text message containing a link to join the Video Chat.

## Joining a Video Chat

The recipient does **not** need to download an app or additional software.

After selecting the link in the text message, they will:

1. Select **Accept and Join**.
2. Allow access to their **camera**.
3. Allow access to their **microphone**.
4. Join the Video Chat directly from their device.

Once selected, a template will pop up (feel free to edit if needed) and go ahead and hit send.

<Check>
  Camera and microphone permissions must be enabled for Video Chat to work properly.
</Check>

## Ways to Use Video Chat

Video Chat can be helpful anytime showing something is easier than explaining it over a text message or phone call.

Some examples include:

- **Share inventory:** Give a prospective customer a virtual walk-around of an inventory item. When using your smartphone, you can flip the camera to show what you're seeing.
- **Virtual service:** Walk through an issue with a customer remotely before determining the next steps.
- **Virtual appointments:** Meet face-to-face with a client, patient, student, or customer without requiring an in-person visit.
- **Service or project walk-throughs:** Show a customer the progress of a job or review completed work.

## Troubleshooting Video Chat

If you or the recipient are having trouble with the camera or audio, first confirm that the browser or device has permission to access the **camera and microphone**.

If **you can see and hear yourself**, but the recipient cannot see or hear you, have the recipient check their camera and microphone permissions on their device.

If **you cannot see or hear yourself**, check your own browser or device permissions.

You can also try:

- Closing and reopening the Video Chat
- Switching to another supported browser, such as Chrome or Safari
- Reviewing the browser or device's camera and microphone permissions
- As a last resort, reinstalling the browser app to reset its permissions
