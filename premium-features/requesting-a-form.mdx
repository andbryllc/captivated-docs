Captivated Forms allow you to send a form directly to a customer's phone by text message. Customers can complete the form from their device, and their submitted form is returned directly to the Captivated conversation.

Forms can be used for a variety of workflows, including:

- Registrations
- Customer intake
- Quote approvals
- Order forms
- Change orders
- Contracts and agreements
- Service authorizations
- Customer acknowledgments
- Other information or approvals your business needs to collect

## Send a Form

To send a form from a conversation:

1. Open the customer conversation.
2. Select the **three-dot menu** below the message field.
3. Choose **Attach Form**.
4. Select the form you'd like to send.
5. Adjust your accompanying message if needed.
6. Select **Send**.

The customer receives a text message with a link to open and complete the form from their phone.

## Send Forms Using Templates

If your team frequently sends the same form, you can attach it to a [Message Template](/features/templates).

This allows your team to quickly send the form along with a consistent message without attaching it manually each time.

## Send Forms Automatically

Forms can also be sent using [Automated Events](/premium-features/automation-events-workflows).

For example, a customer could text a designated keyword and automatically receive the appropriate form.

This can be useful for workflows such as:

- Registrations
- Appointment requests
- Service requests
- Applications
- Intake forms

## Pre-Fill a Form

Some forms allow you to enter information on the customer's behalf before sending it.

For example, you may want to pre-fill:

- Customer information
- Invoice amount
- Quote details
- Order information
- Services included
- Contract details
- Other information already known by your business

To pre-fill an eligible form:

1. Attach the form to the conversation.
2. Select **Pre-Fill**.
3. Review any customer information automatically populated by Captivated.
4. Enter any additional information you'd like to include.
5. Select **Continue**.
6. Review the information before sending the form.

The customer can then complete any remaining fields and submit the form from their phone.

## Form Field Permissions

Forms can be configured to control who is allowed to complete or edit individual fields.

Depending on the form, a field may be:

| Field Setting | Who Can Complete It |
| :-- | :-- |
| **Sender or Recipient** | Your team can pre-fill the field, and the recipient can edit it if needed. |
| **Sender Only** | Your team completes the field and the recipient can view it but cannot change it. |
| **Recipient Only** | The customer must complete the field themselves. |

This is especially helpful for forms where your business needs to provide information for the customer to review before they submit or sign.

## Receive Completed Forms

Once a customer submits a form, the completed submission appears directly within the Captivated conversation.

Select **View PDF** to review the completed form.

Forms can also be configured to automatically email a PDF copy of the completed submission to designated email addresses.

## View Form Submissions

Administrators and other users with appropriate access can review sent and completed forms from the **Form Submissions Dashboard**.

Navigate to:

**Dashboards \> Form Submissions**

From here, you can:

- View sent and submitted forms.
- Filter submissions by date.
- Filter by account or form.
- Preview completed forms.
- Open the Captivated conversation where a form was sent.
- Export submission data to CSV.

Selecting a **submission date** opens the submitted form. Selecting the **sent date** takes you to the Captivated conversation where the form originated.

## Forms & Customer Opt-Ins

Forms can also be configured to collect customer consent for text messaging when appropriate.

The required opt-in language and process should be built into the form based on how your organization intends to communicate with the customer.

<Note>
  Simply submitting a form does not automatically mean a customer has consented to all types of text messaging. Your form must include the appropriate consent language for the messages you intend to send.
</Note>

## Best Practices

- Keep mobile forms as short as possible.
- Only ask customers for information you actually need.
- Pre-fill information you already have to reduce the amount of work required from the customer.
- Review pre-filled information for accuracy before sending.
- Clearly explain what the customer is being asked to complete, approve, or sign.
- Test new forms on a mobile device before sending them to customers.
- Be thoughtful about collecting sensitive information and use the appropriate method for the type of information you're requesting.
