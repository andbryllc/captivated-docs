## Connecting a Payment Provider

Before collecting payments through Express Pay, your organization must have a supported payment provider configured.

Available payment methods may vary depending on your payment provider and account configuration.

If your business uses Authorize.Net, follow our [Connecting Authorize.Net to Captivated](/premium-features/connecting-authorize-net) guide for setup instructions.

## Send a Payment Request

To manually send a payment request:

1. Open the conversation with the contact.
2. Select the **three-dot menu** below the message field.
3. Choose **Request Payment**.
4. Enter the payment details and include a message for the customer.
5. Review the information carefully and select **Send**.

<Warning>
  Review the requested payment amount and payment details before sending the request to your customer.
</Warning>

![](https://learn.captivated.works/wp-content/uploads/2021/04/Screen-Shot-2021-04-29-at-12.21.15-PM.png)

### Add a Payment Request to a Template

If you frequently send the same type of payment request, you can also add one to a [template](/features/templates).

When creating or editing your template, select **Attach a Payment Request**. This allows you to quickly send the payment request along with your templated message.

## What the Customer Sees

The customer will receive a text message containing a secure link to complete their payment.

After opening the link, the customer can choose from the available payment methods, including:

- **Bank account (ACH)**
- **Credit or debit card**
- **Apple Pay**
- **Google Pay**

Express Pay supports Apple Pay and Google Pay directly from a customer's smartphone.

### Paying by Bank Account

If the customer chooses to pay using their bank account, Plaid will guide them through securely connecting their financial institution and selecting the appropriate bank account.

### Paying by Card

If the customer chooses to pay by card, they can enter their card information and complete the payment from their device.

Depending on the device and available payment methods, they may also have the option to use Apple Pay or Google Pay.

## View Payment Status

Payment activity is displayed directly within the Captivated conversation.

Once the customer completes their payment, the payment request will update to show **Paid**.

![](https://learn.captivated.works/wp-content/uploads/2021/04/Screen-Shot-2021-04-29-at-12.24.57-PM.png)

## Cancel a Payment Request

If a payment request has not yet been paid and is no longer needed, select **Cancel Payment Request** within the conversation.

Once a payment is made, the status will change to "Paid." 

![](https://learn.captivated.works/wp-content/uploads/2021/04/Screen-Shot-2021-04-29-at-2.32.08-PM-300x186.png)

## Refund a Payment

Once a payment has been completed, the payment request will display a **Refund** option.

Select **Refund** from the paid payment request to initiate a refund when necessary.

![](https://learn.captivated.works/wp-content/uploads/2021/04/Screen-Shot-2021-04-29-at-2.33.31-PM.png)

## Payments Dashboard

Administrators can review payment activity from the **Payments Dashboard**.

Navigate to:

**Dashboards → Payments**

From the Payments tab, you can:

- Choose a specific date range
- Filter payments by **Paid, Unpaid, Canceled, or Failed**
- Search using a payment's reference number
- Open the corresponding Captivated conversation using the conversation icon
- Initiate a refund using the three-dot menu
- Download payment information to CSV

Each payment has a reference number that can be used to locate and reconcile the transaction.

## Transactions

Select the **Transactions** tab within the Payments Dashboard to review transactions by type.

You can filter and download transaction information or select the conversation icon next to a transaction to open the corresponding conversation.

If a transaction you're looking for isn't displaying, select **Resync** to load the latest transaction information.

## When Will the Money Reach My Bank Account?

Express Pay payouts are deposited into the bank account associated with your payment provider.

Payments typically take approximately **two business days** to process, although actual payout timing may vary depending on your payment provider, financial institution, and account configuration.

## Who Can Send Payment Requests?

Access to Express Pay is permission-based. Payment permissions can be provided to specific users, teams, or the entire organization.

If you don't see the option to request a payment, contact your Captivated Administrator or Captivated Support to confirm your permissions and access to Express Pay.

  <Accordion title="Does Express Pay use our existing credit card processor?">

    Contact Captivated Support to discuss your payment processing options.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How do I know when a customer has paid?">
    The payment status updates directly within the Captivated conversation once the payment has been received.

    You can also review payment activity under **Dashboards → Payments**.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can customers choose which bank account to use?">
    When ACH payments are available through your payment configuration, customers can securely connect and select their bank account using the supported bank authentication process.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can customers use Apple Pay or Google Pay?">
    Apple Pay and Google Pay are supported for eligible payment configurations and compatible devices. Available payment methods may vary by provider.
  </Accordion>
