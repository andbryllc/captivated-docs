## Understanding Conversation Ownership

Every conversation has an **owner**. The owner is the User primarily responsible for the conversation and has additional actions available, including the ability to **Transfer** and **Complete** it.

The conversation owner's avatar appears as the primary User in the conversation.

Other Users can also be **joined** to the conversation without becoming the owner. Joined Users can participate in the conversation and receive notifications, but they cannot Complete or Transfer the conversation.

## Take a Conversation from a Queue

If you're an operator of a [Queue](/administration/admin-queues), new inbound messages may appear in the Queue waiting for someone to take ownership.

To take a conversation, select the **Take** button from the Queue notification.

If Queue previewing is enabled for your User, you can also preview waiting conversations and select the specific conversation you'd like to take.

Once you take a conversation:

- You become the **conversation owner**.
- The conversation is added to your **Active** conversation view.
- You can respond to the contact or transfer the conversation if someone else should handle it.

## Transfer a Conversation

If another User or Queue should handle the conversation, you can transfer ownership.

To transfer a conversation:

1. Open the conversation.
2. Select **Transfer** above the conversation thread.
3. Choose the User or Queue you'd like to transfer the conversation to.

Once transferred, you're no longer the owner and the conversation will be removed from your Active view unless you remain joined.

You can still locate the conversation later using [Conversation Filters and Search](/features/conversation-filtering-and-searching).

<Note>
  Only the conversation owner can transfer a conversation.
</Note>

## Add a User to a Conversation

If you'd like another User to participate **without giving up ownership of the conversation**, add them as a member instead of transferring it.

To add someone:

1. Select **Members** above the conversation.
2. Choose the User you'd like to add.
3. Select **Add**.

The added User becomes joined to the conversation and can participate in the thread.

They can communicate internally using [Internal Notes](/features/adding-an-internal-note) or respond directly to the contact when appropriate.

| If You Want To... | Use |
| :-- | :-- |
| Make another User responsible for the conversation | **Transfer** |
| Keep ownership but have another User participate | **Members** |

## Join a Conversation

Joining a conversation makes you an active participant without changing the conversation owner.

To find a conversation you're not currently joined to, use [Conversation Filters and Search](/features/conversation-filtering-and-searching) to locate it. Depending on your User permissions, you may need to view **Everyone's Conversations** or search for the conversation directly.

Open the conversation and select **Join**.

Once joined:

- The conversation will appear in your Active conversation view.
- You'll receive notifications about activity within the conversation.
- Your User photo will appear in color within the conversation.
- You can participate in the conversation.

## Leave a Conversation

If you're joined to a conversation but no longer need to participate, select **Leave**.

Leaving the conversation:

- Removes you as an active participant.
- Stops notifications from that conversation.
- Does **not** Complete the conversation or change its owner.

If you're not the conversation owner, you'll see **Leave** rather than Complete.

## Complete a Conversation

When you're finished working with a contact, select **Complete** to close the conversation.

Completing conversations helps keep your Active conversation list focused on conversations that still require attention.

<Note>
  Only the conversation owner can Complete a conversation.
</Note>

Completing a conversation **does not delete it**. The entire conversation remains stored in Captivated and can be found using the **Completed** or **All** [Conversation Filters](/features/conversation-filtering-and-searching).

## What Happens After You Complete a Conversation?

What happens when the contact messages your business again depends on how the Channel is configured.

For some Channels, completing a conversation will **unjoin the active Users**. If the contact messages again, the conversation may route back to a [Queue](/administration/admin-queues) for an available operator to handle.

Other Channels may be configured to **keep Users joined after completing**. This is helpful for Users who maintain ongoing relationships with their contacts, such as Sales representatives.

If your User photo remains in color after completing the conversation, you're still joined. You can select **Leave** if you no longer want to remain an active participant.

<Note>
  Your organization's routing and completion settings may vary by Channel. Contact your Captivated Administrator or Captivated Support if you're unsure how your Channels are configured.
</Note>

## Users With Direct Channels

If you have your own direct texting Channel, incoming messages may route directly to you rather than to a Queue.

In this case, completing a conversation does not necessarily change who receives the contact's next message. Future inbound messages will continue to follow the routing configured for that Channel.

## Conversation Actions Quick Reference

| Action | What It Does | Changes Owner? |
| :-- | :-- | :-- |
| **Take** | Takes ownership of a conversation waiting in a Queue | **Yes** |
| **Transfer** | Sends ownership to another User or Queue | **Yes** |
| **Add Member** | Adds another User to participate in the conversation | No |
| **Join** | Makes you an active participant | No |
| **Leave** | Removes you as an active participant | No |
| **Complete** | Closes the conversation and removes it from the active workflow | No |

## Need Help?
