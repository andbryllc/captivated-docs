---
title: "Sending a Group Message"
description: "Captivated allows you to send and receive group text messages with up to 10 people. Everyone included in the group can see messages and replies from the other participants."
---

There are two ways to start a group message:

1. Using the **green New Message button**
2. From the **Contacts** section

<Note>
  Group messages are different from [Announcements](/features/announcements). Group message participants can see and respond to messages from everyone in the group, while Announcements are sent BCC and each recipient receives the message individually.
</Note>

## Start a Group Message Using the New Message Button

Select the green **New Message** button in the top right-hand corner of Captivated.

Make sure you are on the **SMS** option and begin selecting your contacts. Once you select more than one contact, the option to send a group message will appear.

Continue selecting the contacts you'd like to include. Remember, a group message can include **no more than 10 people**.

## Start a Group Message from Contacts

You can also create a group message from the **Contacts** section in the left-hand menu.

Select the checkbox next to each contact you'd like to include. Once more than one contact is selected, choose **Send a Group Message**.

## Select Your Sending Channel

After choosing your contacts, a new screen will appear where you can:

- Review the contacts and phone numbers included in the group
- Choose the Captivated channel/phone number you'd like to send the message from

If a contact has opted out of messages from the selected channel, you will not be able to continue with that contact included.

You can either:

- Select the red remove icon to remove that contact from the group, or
- Choose a different channel that the contact is eligible to receive messages from

Once all contacts are selected and eligible to receive messages from the chosen channel, select **Continue**.

## Send Your Group Message

Type your message in the **Write a Message** field or select an existing [template](/features/templates).

You can also select the **info icon** in the upper right-hand corner to:

- View the phone numbers included in the group
- Give your group conversation a name (optional)

Once you're ready, send your message as usual.

## If Someone Opts Out

<Info>
  If one of the contacts in an existing group message opts out, you will no longer be able to continue messaging within that group thread.
</Info>

To continue communicating with the remaining contacts, you will need to create a **new group message** without the opted-out contact.

## Group Message Limit

Group messages are limited to **10 people**. If you need to send the same message to a larger group, consider using [Announcements](/features/announcements), if that feature is enabled for your account and your recipients meet the applicable opt-in requirements.
