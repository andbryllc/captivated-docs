If a message cannot be delivered, Captivated will display a **Message Failed** indicator with the message.

## View the Failure Reason

To see why a message failed:

1. Locate the **Message Failed** indicator on the failed message.
2. Open the dropdown next to the indicator.
3. Select **View Error**.
4. Review the **error code and failure reason** provided.

The error details can help you determine why the carrier was unable to deliver the message and what action, if any, you should take.

![](https://learn.captivated.works/wp-content/uploads/2023/12/Blog-5-2.png)

## Common Reasons Messages Fail

Messages can fail for a variety of reasons, including:

- The phone number cannot receive text messages.
- The number is inactive or no longer in service.
- The recipient has opted out or blocked messaging.
- A carrier or network was temporarily unable to deliver the message.
- The message was filtered by a carrier.
- The message contained content or a link that triggered carrier filtering.
- There is a restriction associated with the sender, recipient, or messaging campaign.

![](https://learn.captivated.works/wp-content/uploads/2023/12/Blog-6.png)

<Tip>
  If you're unsure whether the contact's number can receive texts, check the [SMS Verification badge](/features/sms-verification) on the contact record.
</Tip>

## What Should I Do If a Message Fails?

Start by reviewing the error displayed under **View Error**. In some cases, the reason will clearly identify the issue and no additional troubleshooting is necessary.

If the error isn't clear, messages continue to fail unexpectedly, or you believe a message should have been delivered, contact **Captivated Support**.

When contacting Support, provide the affected conversation and failed message whenever possible. Our team can review the error and determine whether additional troubleshooting or carrier investigation is necessary.

<Note>
  Some delivery failures occur outside of Captivated and may require investigation by our carrier partners. Captivated Support will determine the appropriate next steps based on the error.
</Note>

## Need Help?

If you need help interpreting a failed message or error code, contact **Captivated Support** using the in-app **Support** option.
