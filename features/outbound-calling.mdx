## Call Forwarding

If Captivated has provided your business with a designated texting line, an Administrator should configure call forwarding so that customers who **call the texting number** are directed to the appropriate phone number.

When someone calls the Captivated text line, the call will automatically forward to the number entered in the **Forward Inbound Calls To** field.

<Note>
  Calls can only be forwarded to **one U.S. phone number at a time**. An Administrator can update the forwarding number whenever needed.
</Note>

For example, if your organization rotates after-hours or on-call responsibilities, an Administrator can update the forwarding number to route calls to the appropriate person.

### Set Up Call Forwarding

1. Navigate to **Administration → Channels**.
2. Locate the channel you want to configure.
3. Select **Edit Channel**.
4. Enter the appropriate phone number in **Forward Inbound Calls To**.
5. Select **Save**.

![Input forwarding number](https://learn.captivated.works/wp-content/uploads/2018/08/Screenshot-2023-09-11-at-2.38.31-PM-1.png)

Once saved, the channel details will display the number where inbound calls are being forwarded.

<Check>
  If your organization changes the phone number receiving inbound calls, remember to update the forwarding number in the channel settings.
</Check>

## Outbound Calling

Captivated's outbound calling feature allows you to call a contact directly from a Captivated conversation.

This can be used whether Captivated has text-enabled an existing business landline or your business uses a dedicated Captivated texting line.

When you initiate an outbound call:

1. Captivated first calls the **personal mobile number listed in your profile**.
2. Answer the incoming call.
3. Captivated then connects the call to your customer.
4. The **business number displays as the caller ID** to the customer rather than your personal mobile number.

This allows you to make business calls using your personal phone without sharing your personal number with the customer.

## Make an Outbound Call

From the conversation with the contact:

1. Select the **more menu** below the message field.
2. Choose **Call Contact**.
3. Captivated will call the mobile number associated with your profile.
4. Answer the call.
5. Captivated will then connect you to the contact.

![Call Contact option](https://learn.captivated.works/wp-content/uploads/2020/05/Screen-Shot-2020-05-06-at-5.12.16-PM.png)

<Check>
  Make sure your phone isn't configured to automatically silence or block unknown callers. You must be able to answer the initial call from Captivated for the outbound call to connect.
</Check>

## Add Your Mobile Number

To make outbound calls from Captivated, your personal cell phone number must be entered in the **Mobile Phone Number** field of your profile.

Navigate to your **avatar → My Profile** and enter your personal cell phone number in the appropriate field.

![Mobile phone number field](https://learn.captivated.works/wp-content/uploads/2018/08/Screenshot-2023-09-11-at-3.06.46-PM.png)

<Note>
  Your personal mobile number is used to connect the outbound call. The customer sees the business number as the caller ID, not your personal mobile number.
</Note>

## Don't See the Call Contact Option?

If **Call Contact** isn't available in your conversation menu, the feature may not be enabled for your organization.

Contact **Captivated Support** using the **Support** option in the left-hand menu for assistance.
