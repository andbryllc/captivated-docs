---
title: "Number Masking"
description: "Number masking provides an additional layer of privacy for the contacts stored in Captivated by limiting the visibility of their phone numbers."
---

When number masking is enabled, all but the last four digits of a contact's phone number are hidden once the contact has been added to Captivated.

For example:

(317) 555-1234 → •••-•••-1234

## Why Use Number Masking?

Number masking can help your organization:

- **Protect customer privacy** by limiting access to full phone numbers.
- **Protect your contact data** by preventing Users from easily accessing or copying customer phone numbers, including when an employee leaves your organization.

## Enable Number Masking

Number masking can be enabled for your business **at no additional charge**.

To turn it on, contact **Captivated Support** using the **Support** option in the left-hand menu and request that number masking be enabled for your account.
